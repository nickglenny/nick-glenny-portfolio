## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I tried Meta ads and they didn't work." | Acknowledge. "Most fail at the follow-up, not the ad. The ad gets the lead — the SMS in the next 5 minutes books the trial. Without that, you're paying for leads that ghost." Pair with proof. |
| "How is this different from [Loud Rumor / Gym Launch]?" | Founder-led, niche-specific, you keep the ad account, no 12-month contract, integrates with your existing booking software (not their proprietary funnel). |
| "I don't have budget for ads on top of your fee." | Reframe: "Most studios spend $X on filled trials. The package is designed to pay back in [Y weeks] from a single new member. Math: …" |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | [Risk reversal — guarantee structure if you offer one, or specific milestones with off-ramps.] |
| "I'm not technical — will I have to learn ad manager?" | No. Weekly Loom + simple report. You approve creative, I run everything. |
| "Can you work with [Mindbody / Mariana Tek / ClubReady / Zen Planner]?" | [Yes — name the integrations you actually do.] |
