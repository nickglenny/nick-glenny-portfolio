follow-up process.

An outreach assistant that uses account history and studio rules to help prepare the right follow-up.

Screen walkthrough with commentary

2:38

The work

I’m developing this around how Carrie’s handles lead follow-up. The workflow brings together contact logs, recent conversations and account details to help decide who needs a message and what it should say.

Where AI helps

Reviewing that context and preparing drafts for approval. I’m building the process and review tools around the studio’s rules, with a person checking the wording before a message goes out.

The step between “this person is due” and “this is the right message to send.”

Inside the content calendar in Asana

The work

I give AI a batch of photos and the context for the month. We work through image choices, caption drafts and ideas tied to studio events, holidays and the people in the community.

Where AI helps

Turning that plan into carousels, reel ideas, birthday posts and other creative, then helping with content organization and posting automation. I guide the direction and review the work.

A repeatable process that connects the photos, the caption and the reason for the post.
