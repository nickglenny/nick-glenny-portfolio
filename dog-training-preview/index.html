What social media does for the business

When someone hears about a dog trainer, the first thing they do is look them up. Social media is what they find. It works like a shop window that's open all day.

People trust you before they call

Short videos of real sessions show how you work with dogs and owners. By the time someone books, they already feel like they know you.

Word of mouth goes further

Happy clients can share a video of their own dog with friends in one tap. Every share is a referral that keeps working on its own.

Pack walks launch with people already waiting

If people follow the van being built, they're excited for it before it's finished. The first walks can fill from people who signed up early.

The luxury shows

A service like this is chosen on look and feel. A consistent, polished account with the same colors, type and photo style on every post tells people this isn't the average dog walker.

Every post points to one simple action: book a free consultation.

Reel: a short vertical video, usually 15 to 60 seconds. Instagram shows these to people who don't follow you yet, so they're the main way new people find you. Story: a quick photo or clip that disappears after a day. Good for "out on a walk right now" moments.

What we'd post

You mentioned not knowing what to post. You already have plenty. It falls into four groups, and we rotate between them so the account always has something new.

Training

Show the work

  • A series on each of the five training levels

  • On-the-spot fixes, like pulling on the leash
  • "Homework" tips owners can practice at home
The van

Follow the build

  • Before photos and the plan for it

Pack walks

  • How to get on the list
Clients

Let happy owners talk

  • Short clips of owners describing the change
  • A "dog of the week" from your current clients
  • Meet the trainer: who you are and how you work
  • Answers to questions people always ask
