01

CLUB EXPERIENCE

From front door to first rally

Let someone who’s never visited see the club through a player’s eyes.

View club-tour reel reference

Megan Fudge’s Pickleball Cayman club reel. Format inspiration; Sandy would use its own footage.

Open filming walkthrough

Sandy version: Follow one adult player from arrival to a game.

  1. Film a clean shot of the entrance or club sign.
  2. Capture the player walking in with their paddle.
  3. Get a wide view of the courts from a safe position.
  4. Film a few seconds of a rally, then a paddle tap.

Send: 5–7 vertical clips, each about 5 seconds. Keep a little extra footage at each end.

I handle: Clip selection, pacing, music, on-screen words, cover, caption, and publishing.

Possible opening: “Your next game starts here.”

02

MEMBER VOICES

Describe the club in one word

Give members an easy prompt and let their personalities carry the reel.

Watch Nick’s member reel

Existing Fountain Valley work. A reference for the interview style and finished edit.

Open filming walkthrough

Sandy version: Ask 4–6 adult members: “How would you describe The Picklr Sandy?”

  1. Ask willing members between games.
  2. Film each person vertically in a quieter part of the club.
  3. Keep the phone close enough to hear a clear answer.
  4. Get two or three short clips of those members playing.

Send: The original answers and supporting clips. Don’t trim the answers first.

I handle: The opening question, strongest answers, speech captions, supporting clips, cover, and post copy.

Possible ending: “What’s your word?”

03

COACH SPOTLIGHT / ALTERNATE

One tip before your next game

Introduce the coaching team with one useful, approachable takeaway.

Watch Nick’s Coach Dylan reel

Existing RSM work. A reference for filming a coach on court.

Open filming walkthrough

Sandy version: A coach chooses one beginner question they hear often.

  1. Ask the coach to introduce the question in one sentence.
  2. Film their short answer facing the phone.
  3. Record the coach demonstrating the point from the side.
  4. Capture one clean example of the shot or drill.

Send: One talking clip and 2–3 demonstration clips. Leave background music off while recording.

I handle: Tightening the answer, matching the demonstration, speech captions, cover, and scheduling.

Before posting: The coach checks the final instructional point.

Fresh references each week.

03 / EVENTS & WEEKLY RECAPS

Keep the club in the conversation.

Support the next event and give members a reason to check back each week.

Stories around an event

The sequence changes with the event. Each frame has one job.

  1. 01

    Announce it

    What’s happening, who it’s for, and the confirmed date and time.

  2. 02

    Make booking easy

    A short reminder with the approved registration link. Include availability only when verified.

  3. 03

    Bring people into the moment

    A day-of reminder or a few club clips, followed by a recap when footage is available.

A weekly recap people can use

A static post or carousel combining recent club moments with the next week’s priorities.

  • Highlight a few member or program moments.
  • Pull in confirmed upcoming events and sessions.
  • Point people to the right place to book.

Sandy supplies the schedule and photos. I organize the post, write the copy, and publish it using the approved asset process.
