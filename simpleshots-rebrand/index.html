Slide 02 · The brief and brand goals

Real juice.
Real spirits.
Nothing fake.

SimpleShots is a spiked juice shot: organic fruit juice plus a premium spirit, founded by two best friends in Southern California. The brand book calls for something flavor-first, transparent and playful, made for people who actually drink today. The site had to carry that in the first scroll.

Goal 01
Lead with the claims

The nutrition label is the reason the brand exists. Every page states it in three lines.

Goal 02
Feel like the brand book

Palette, type, hand icons and packaging cues carried from print to screen without a generic theme in between.

Goal 03
Sell where people buy

One primary action, Shop on Instacart, repeated down the page, with Find near you as the backup.

Why it works the brand book specifies Fig Grotesk and Roc Grotesk. Slide 04 · Logo, icons and voice

The hand does
the talking.

The brand book's logo system is a wordmark plus a hand-drawn "hand holding a shot" icon, with cheers, lemon and lime variants. On the site they replace every generic UI icon: the claims stickers, the age gate, the promise list. Nothing off-the-shelf sits next to the brand.

Claims are styled as stickers: offset shadow, tilted slightly, one brand color each. They read as packaging, which is where customers first meet the claims.

One tagline, everywhere: hero, footer, meta description. Playful, claim-led, and it explains the product in eight words.

Organic juice + premium spirits

Mono uppercase labels carry the factual voice (ABV, calories, ingredients) so the display type can stay loud without the page feeling like an ad.

Slide 05 · Photography and layout

Big photos. Blocks of color.
Not much else.

137 shots from the brand session: pool days, flat-lays, the founders, the bottle in hand. The layout is built to show them large and let each section change color underneath.

Product cards wear their flavor. Cranberry Crisp on Berry, Pineapple Gingerita on Olive, each with a 400px photo gallery. The Instacart button fills with the card's accent so the primary action never gets lost.

Split sections alternate photo and color. Promise, lifestyle and founders blocks each pair one big image with one block of Ink, Rose or Cream, so scrolling feels like flipping through the brand book.

Mobile keeps the stickers. Claims stack under the headline, product cards go full-width, photos stay tall. No decorative rules or eyebrow dashes anywhere; the photos are the decoration.

Slide 06 · Alternatives and the system

We never hand over
one option.

Every decision shipped with alternatives the client can switch on in the Shopify editor: three heroes, three promise sections, five style packs. They pick what suits them, and nothing they did not pick is thrown away.

Style pack · Editorial

Serif italic headline, quieter chrome.

Motion

Word-by-word headline reveals (55ms stagger), scroll reveals on blocks and photos, subtle magnetic buttons. 700ms ease-out curve. Everything off under reduced-motion.

Build

Shopify Dawn 16 with 20+ custom sections, all editable in the theme customizer. A schema lint catches the Shopify limits that silently break sections before every upload.

Style packs

Brand, Editorial, Pop, Night and Coastal: one theme setting swaps fonts and tokens across the whole store, so the client can test a direction without a rebuild.
